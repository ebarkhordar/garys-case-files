Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Who we are

Gary's Case Files is a video channel run by Ehsan Barkhordar.

What our app does

Our publishing tool, ai-videos, signs in to Google with the channel owner's own account. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload our videos and set their titles and descriptions, and the YouTube Analytics API to read our own videos' statistics.

Whose data

Only our own channel's. The tool isn't offered to anyone else, and it doesn't collect, store or process data about any other Google user.

What we store

An OAuth token for our own account, kept on our own server and our own computer and readable only by us. We don't sell, share or transfer it.

YouTube and Google

The tool uses YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

The account owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
