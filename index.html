Gary's Case Files

Short animated videos in which Gary, a pigeon building inspector, solves one true engineering case in under a minute: why a London tower melted a car, why the Leaning Tower of Pisa hasn't fallen, and more.

Watch on YouTube and Instagram.

How the videos are published

We write and animate every episode ourselves. A small tool of ours, ai-videos, uploads each finished video to our own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API and reads our own videos' statistics through the YouTube Analytics API. It acts only on our own channel and isn't offered to anyone else.
